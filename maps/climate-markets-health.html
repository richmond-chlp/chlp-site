What the map shows

  • Outdoor air is the pathway that carbon markets reach most often. Diet, physical activity and heat action plans draw their main money from public budgets, with no traded unit.
  • Several interventions earn credits or subsidies while their health sign is harmful or unresolved. In each crediting method that CHLP read, health is left out or optional.
  • One unit on the left pays for a health outcome directly: Gold Standard certifies averted DALYs for clean cooking. Every other traded unit pays for a ton, a kilowatt-hour or a liter of fuel.
  • The Pathfinder review finds the largest modeled health effects in clean cooking and diet change. Clean cooking rests on 2 of its 200 actions and diet on 91, and 89% of all 200 are modeled. Diet change has no market, and clean cooking has the market's most disputed credits.
  • Rules and standards drive several interventions and pay nothing. Some regulators count health when they write a rule, and crediting methods rarely do.
  • Each ton avoided also lowers future climate damage to health. That effect belongs to every row, so the map leaves it out and CHLP's tool reads it through its bridge rate.

  • How the signs were set. A sign follows a CHLP assessment where one exists and the Pathfinder evidence elsewhere. The signs for infection by insects follow Kibret for dams and are judgments elsewhere. Each remaining sign is a judgment and is open to correction. Most published health estimates are modeled, and few are measured. The Pathfinder review holds no study from a low-income country, and India is its only lower middle-income one. A sign can differ by place, and the map gives one sign for each link. Each intervention’s panel names the source behind each sign, with a link where one exists, and says so where a sign is a judgment. A source read only in part, or only as an abstract or a summary, is marked.
  • How main and minor were set. By judgment, checked against the volumes found. A published figure matched 37 of the 91 links to a traded unit or to finance, and the rest have none. Finance figures count money from every source for a whole sector, so they overstate what reaches one intervention.
  • What the map leaves out. The size of each health effect, a volume for most links, and mental health, which map 4 traces across every focus area.
