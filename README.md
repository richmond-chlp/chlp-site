# The Climate Health Ledger Project

The source of [climatehealthledger.org](https://climatehealthledger.org/), the home page of The Climate Health Ledger Project (CHLP).

CHLP builds open tools that estimate how much a climate program improves people's health for each dollar spent, and maps of the money behind climate interventions and their routes to health. It will check shared results against implementers' own records.

- **The tools:** the [Program Estimator](https://tool.climatehealthledger.org/) and the [Funder's Ledger](https://tool.climatehealthledger.org/chlp-funder-ledger.html), both in open beta.
- **The maps:** the [Intervention Maps](https://climatehealthledger.org/maps/), five maps of climate, animal welfare and food interventions and their routes to health, in open beta.
- **Contact:** richmond@climatehealthledger.org

Each page is a single HTML file; the landing page also shows one image of the Program Estimator's result. Nothing on the site sends or stores what a visitor types.
